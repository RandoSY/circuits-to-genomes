## Paper 1 v10: add a parallel-workload extension after §6.1, revise §7 synthesis

Proposed manuscript text: To investigate whether the architectural trade-off extends beyond sequential subroutine dispatch and synthetic Boolean networks, we constructed a logical parallel matrix-product model motivated by Hopper's hierarchical programming environment. In the initial model (pilot v0.1), combining localized standby resources with algebraic recovery reduced staged operand copies from 64 to 16 and mean equal-site disruption from 1.00 to 0.615 outputs, while increasing worst-case disruption from one to eight and matrix products by 12.5%. These resource and fault metrics are not equivalent to measured device throughput, power or failure rates. In v0.2 we held nominal staged operand counts and product counts constant across four logical designs and changed the dependencies of checking and recovery. A single corrupt staged operand produced eight wrong unflagged outputs when parity shared that operand; separately staging the parity operand flagged the whole affected group. A common upstream corruption still escaped detection unless the checker received an independently injectable logical root. Identical corruption of both roots, shared corruption of the other operand, unavailable checks, and canceling errors defeated relevant protections. The result illustrates that the topology of protective dependencies matters beyond resource count, but follows from explicit model assumptions. It neither establishes a universal law nor demonstrates a gain on H100 hardware. Established algorithm-based fault tolerance is the engineering baseline. Actual CUDA performance and physical reliability remain separate tests.

Proposed H1-D (prospective, not preregistered here): at fixed effective protection budget and comparable workload, reducing common-cause dependencies decreases undetected disruption for faults in those dependencies; the advantage may disappear for common upstream faults, ambiguous fault localization, or excessive coordination overhead. Test against full duplication, weighted checksums, established ABFT, and independent-source baselines. Report resource cost vector, available outputs, detected wrong outputs, undetected wrong outputs, worst case, and fault strata separately. No H2 rescue, no biological economy claim, no H3 system-level test. Preserve Paper 1's negative K562 result and H3-M limits.

## Paper 2 v1.2: add prospective shared-dependency hypothesis to Discussion

Proposed manuscript text: The parallel-computation model suggests separating the number of protective elements from the diversity of their upstream dependencies. Multiple enhancers associated with the same target need not be independently responsive to perturbation: they may share transcription-factor requirements or chromatin context. An experimentally tractable hypothesis is that, among co-active enhancers with comparable count and baseline activity, lower overlap of functionally supported upstream regulator dependencies predicts less target-output disruption under a perturbation of one regulator. This is distinct from individual-element reporter equivalence. It does not imply that enhancer regulation implements checksums. The negative locus-level multiplicity result in v1.1 remains negative; the proposed dependency-overlap hypothesis is a new question and cannot be retroactively substituted for the original hypothesis.

### Prospective protocol (freeze before looking at outcome data)

Unit: target gene × matched cell state × regulator perturbation, not individual reporter barcode or enhancer tile. Start with validated enhancer-target assignments and multiple co-active enhancers in a consistent cell context. Define upstream dependency using perturbation evidence where available; motifs/PWM scores are proxies and cannot establish occupancy or causal necessity. Define pairwise weighted Jaccard overlap on regulator-dependency profiles, aggregate to a prespecified mean pairwise score for targets with >=2 elements. Define response as absolute log2 change in target expression after a defined TF perturbation; prespecify practical margin and uncertainty. Compare baseline (enhancer count, activity, distance, target expression, regulator expression, cell type and technical covariates) vs baseline + overlap, using held-out genes/chromosomes and an independent dataset if available. Cluster by target and perturbation; no barcode pseudoreplication. Test interaction with perturbation target and sensitivity to assignments/TF-family similarity; account for multiple testing. Negative controls: shuffled dependency labels preserving counts and target-degree structure; perturbations unrelated to nominated dependencies. Falsifier: no improvement out of sample or a robust opposite direction. Alternative explanations: accessibility, pleiotropy, target expression, assay precision, shared chromatin and annotation error. No new dataset has been selected or validated here. The existing GSE110760 paired reporter assay can motivate motif features, but it cannot by itself identify endogenous shared-regulator dependencies or target gene perturbation responses. Ask an evolutionary geneticist and statistician to approve the design before running it.

## Paper 3 v0.5: extend §11 and §12; add a research episode

Proposed manuscript text: Version 0.4 described the return direction as a proposed investigation. The subsequent v0.1 and v0.2 parallel-computation pilots provide a first documented implementation of that agenda. The investigator asked whether analysis of the PDP-8 and regulatory function could be extended to a modern parallel workload. AI-assisted modeling exposed a case in which data and verification agreed despite shared corruption. Separating dependencies at one layer improved detection, while a common upstream source or canceling errors defeated the check. This supplies an illustrative analogy for the research process itself: agreement between AI systems is not necessarily independent corroboration when they inherit common datasets, code or assumptions. The computational experiment does not measure model error correlation or the effectiveness of multi-AI review. Its role is to generate a falsifiable question and refine the verification protocol. It is an instance of reciprocal inquiry at the level of research activity, not proof of a new biological mechanism or engineering advance.

Proposed extension to the accountability gate: record a dependency map for every important claim (primary evidence, data version, analytical code, assumptions, reviewers, outputs); identify common dependencies and what would cause all checks to fail together. Require genuinely different error-detection paths when material to the conclusion, e.g. independent implementation, negative controls, blinded qualified review, independent measurement. Distinguish "checked", "independently reproduced", "externally reviewed" and "supported"; an absent or failed check does not mean a result has passed. Preserve dissent and negative outcomes. Prospective educational study: matched tasks with seeded errors in shared source vs separate implementation, counterbalanced single-AI, multiple-AI shared-source and independent-source review conditions. Blinded expert score of erroneous claims detected, valid claims rejected, explanation quality, reviewer time, and final calibration; consent, ethics approval, independent scoring and prespecified design required. No outcome from this study is claimed.
