# Prospective analysis protocol — H2O2-FC v0.1
27 November 2026. Author-directed Option C; exploratory prior data separated from future confirmation. No registration claim. No wet-lab genetic work proposed: use existing public data and safe educational demonstration separately.

## Central construct
Functional continuity = preservation of a specified performance envelope across changes in implementation, not merely annotation or identical chemical reaction. Separate reaction identity, enzyme kinetic performance, cellular peroxide balance, growth under exposure, and evolutionary history. A similar phenotype can be convergent, not inherited. A molecular system may tolerate rather than remove some peroxide; signaling is not damage.

## Stage 2 — enzyme structure and evolutionary variation (H-P1)
Primary endpoint: log10 measured observed maximum rate per heme per second for typical heme catalases in matched assay strata; secondary specific activity, half-maximum concentration and thermal stability kept distinct. Screen availability of exact accession and construct, species, assay temperature/pH, heme occupancy, substrate concentration, uncertainty, and structure identity. Include low-activity homologs when measured. Do not pool disparate units. Start with CAT-FEM exact accession stage and original data. Use global BLOSUM62 alignments within homologous families; record coverage, gaps, sequence version. Prespecify catalytic residue conservation, channel geometry and oligomeric-interface features with mechanistic justification. Compare M0 assay-only/global baseline, M1 sequence+phylogeny baseline, M2 M1+structure. Split by sequence-related group and study before fitting; avoid homolog leakage, post-hoc threshold adjustment and treating pairs as independent. Report held-out MAE, uncertainty at independent group level, and performance on independent matched data. Freeze modeling decisions before examining new endpoints. Feasibility gate: if insufficient matched independent groups, publish a feasibility report, not a predictive claim. No arbitrary universal equivalence margin; a tolerance must be justified for a defined endpoint.

## Stage 3 — alternative implementation and systems protection (H-P2)
Primary organism-level endpoint candidate: growth rate relative to matched unstressed control at defined peroxide exposure, with intracellular peroxide measurement as a separate mechanistic endpoint. First seek existing matched isogenic perturbation panels with single and combined loss of catalase/thiol-dependent scavengers and controls, baseline growth, exposure duration, and biological replicates. No claim of experimental equivalence from disk-zone summary. Compare architecture-aware predictors (condition-specific functional complementarity, regulation, reducing-equivalent dependencies, compartmentation) against baseline with total measured scavenging capacity, exposure, organism and assay conditions. For available factorial datasets fit interaction contrasts on prespecified response scale; avoid interpreting nonadditivity alone as shared pathway or compensation. Include null effects and failed protection. Group held-out studies/lineages; if available evidence is only one organism, claim within-system result only. Test whether architecture adds predictive information under changing exposure, not whether enzyme count alone correlates with resistance. Opposite or null predictive gain counts against hypothesis. Do not extrapolate mutant compensation to historical evolution without phylogenetic evidence.

## Stage 4 — evolutionary inference (H-P3; contingent)
Curate verified ortholog/paralog families, organism oxygen ecology and phylogeny; distinguish heme catalase, catalase-peroxidase, Mn catalase, peroxiredoxins and glutathione peroxidases. No concatenated alignment across nonhomologous families. Account for horizontal transfer, gene loss, duplication, gene annotation uncertainty, shared ancestry and growth environment. Presence is not activity or sufficiency. Where comparable phenotypes exist, compare functional-envelope retention with changes in measured dependencies, using phylogenetically informed model and independent lineages. If no adequate data, remain descriptive. Do not date LUCA or oxygenation from enzyme presence alone.

## Release gates
1. Source and construct audit; exact public accession manifest, raw sources and transformations.
2. Freeze primary endpoints, inclusion rules, grouping and inference before outcome modeling; expert review.
3. Reproducible scripts and positive/negative controls; report unsuccessful tests.
4. Independent external validation or explicit limitation; no major discovery claim based on descriptive anchors.
5. Independent enzyme/oxidative-stress and biostatistics reviewer; author approval before submission.
